# Prompt لإعادة توليد الفيديو برمية شال منطقية

Static camera, plain beige room, cardboard boxes on the right, loaded barbell on the floor.
A man in a beige t-shirt and black pants lifts a cream scarf with a lanyard off his neck
and pulls it over his head. He holds the scarf loosely in his right hand, swings his arm
sideways in one smooth motion and RELEASES it. The scarf stays visible the whole time:
it leaves the hand, unfolds, falls under gravity in a natural arc to the left, and lands
on the floor beside the barbell, resting there for the rest of the clip. No teleporting,
no vanishing, no hovering. His arm relaxes after the release, then he bends down and
grips the barbell for a deadlift.

Negative: scarf disappearing mid-air, floating, changing shape, clipping through the body.

# Prompt: عدة تكرارات بش أب حقيقية

Same room and static camera. After placing the scarf on the floor, the man lowers into a
plank on his hands beside the barbell and performs FIVE continuous, full push-ups at a steady
pace: chest down to a few centimeters above the floor, back straight, then a full lockout.
Each repetition is smooth and slightly different in speed, with natural breathing.
One continuous shot, no cuts.

Negative: jump cuts, repeated identical loops, ghosting, morphing body parts.
